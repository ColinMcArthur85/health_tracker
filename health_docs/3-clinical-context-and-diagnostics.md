# Clinical Context and Diagnostic Reinterpretation

This document clarifies recent lab "red flags" using the context of lifestyle, illness, and regional lab access (BC, Canada).

## 1. Reinterpreting the 2025 "Storm" (CRP/WBC/Ferritin)
The elevated CRP (15.7), high WBC, and doubled Ferritin occurred during a period of:
*   **Acute Illness:** Confirmed "body went through the wringer" for 2 weeks.
*   **Sleep Deprivation:** 6 and 4-year-old kids + "pretty shite" sleep for years.
*   **Stress:** High family stress.

**Attia Conclusion:** These values are "noise," not "signal." CRP and WBC must be measured when the subject is 100% well to be useful for longevity risk stratification.

## 2. Ferritin vs. Iron
*   **True Iron Status:** Requires Ferritin + Serum Iron + Transferrin Saturation.
*   **Inflammatory Rise:** Because Ferritin rises with inflammation, your 121 µg/L result is likely a "reactive" rise due to the illness that spiked your CRP.
