# Metabolic and Organ Health Summary (Longevity Lens)

Based on longitudinal GP and Naturopath labs (2020–2025) and cross-referenced with Peter Attia’s "Four Horsemen" framework.

## 1. Glycemic Control & Metabolic Health
**Status: Strong Strength**

*   **HbA1c:** Stable and optimal.
    *   2020 & 2021: 4.8%
    *   2025: 5.2% (Still well within the non-diabetic range).
*   **Triglycerides (2025):** 0.91 mmol/L (Excellent).
*   **HDL:** 1.46 mmol/L (Solid).
*   **Attia Framing:** Suggests low short-term risk of Type 2 Diabetes and good insulin sensitivity. A1c < 5.3% with low triglycerides is highly reassuring.

## 2. Kidney Function
**Status: Good**

*   **Creatinine:** Stable and normal across all years.
*   **eGFR:** Consistently >80, often >100.
*   **Urine Albumin/Creatinine:** <2.0 mg/mmol (Normal).
*   **Attia Framing:** Absence of microalbuminuria is a favorable signal for long-term vascular and kidney health.

## 3. Liver Health
**Status: Good**

*   **Enzymes:** ALT and ALP are normal.
*   **Indication:** No standard lab evidence of Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD), which Attia considers a major metabolic risk factor.

## 4. Testosterone (Baseline)
**Status: Acceptable (Mid-Range)**

*   **Data (2020):**
    *   Total Testosterone: 12.8 nmol/L.
    *   Free Testosterone: 313 pmol/L.
    *   SHBG: 22.9 nmol/L (Favorable for bioavailability).
*   **Attia Framing:** Adequate for an active individual. While not "elite," it is not concerning in isolation provided the individual is asymptomatic.
