# Hybrid Training Plan (Strength, Mobility, Stability)

A 3-on / 1-off rotation designed for home training with minimal equipment (Kettlebells, Med Ball, Paralettes).

## 1. Programming Sources
*   **Strength & Conditioning:** [Samuel Jordan Fitness](https://www.youtube.com/@SamuelJordanFitness)
*   **Mobility & Movement:** [EDR Fitness](https://www.youtube.com/@edrfitness)
*   **Foundational Pillars:** Peter Attia (Stability, Strength, Zone 2, Zone 5).

## 2. Rotation Schedule (3 On / 1 Off)

| Day | Workout Type | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | **Kettlebell S&C** | High volume, complexes, metabolic conditioning (Samuel Jordan style). |
| **Day 2** | **Mobility / Rehab** | Joint health, thoracic spine, hip mobility, movement quality (EDR style). |
| **Day 3** | **Attia Strength** | Low rep, high tension, stability-focused movements. |
| **Day 4** | **Rest** | Active recovery (Walking), sleep priority, stress management. |

## 3. Sample "Attia Strength" Day (Home-Focus)
Aimed at max strength and quality of movement.
*   **Pull:** Inverted rows (Paralette bars) — 3 sets to tech failure.
*   **Hinge:** Single-leg KB RDLs — 3 x 8 each side.
*   **Push:** Dips or elevated Push-ups — 3 sets.
*   **Core:** Pallof Press (KB or Med Ball) — 3 x 10.
*   **Stability:** Med Ball Carries or Wall Sits — Max effort.

## 4. Equipment Progression
*   Current: Kettlebells, Med Ball, Paralettes.
*   Goal: Eventually transition to heavier bells (strength is the most important metric for muscle mass retention as we age).

## 5. Non-Negotiables
*   **Sleep Quality:** No training if sleep is < 6 hours of poor quality.
*   **Form over Load:** Use slow eccentrics (lowering phase) to increase difficulty without needing massive weights.
