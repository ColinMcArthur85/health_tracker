# Action Plan and Next Steps

Priority items to bridge the gap between current labs and an "Attia-style" longevity profile.

## Priority 1: The "Clean Baseline" Labs
Wait until you are well, rested, and in a lower-stress window to run these.
*   **hs-CRP:** High-sensitivity C-Reactive Protein (confirm recovery from illness).
*   **Ferritin + Serum Iron + Transferrin Sat:** Verify if Ferritin returns to <70 µg/L.

## Priority 2: Advanced Lipid Panel
Crucial for determining actual cardiovascular risk.
*   **ApoB:** (The gold standard for particle count).
*   **Lp(a):** (One-time test to rule out genetic high-risk baseline).

## Priority 3: Metabolic Deep Dive
Move beyond A1c to see early signs of insulin resistance.
*   **Fasting Insulin:** To calculate HOMA-IR (Fasting Glucose x Fasting Insulin).
*   **OGTT (Optional):** Oral Glucose Tolerance Test if concerns arise.

## Priority 4: Fitness Benchmarks (Subjective/Wearable)
*   **VO2 Max:** Estimate via 1.5-mile run or wearable (Garmin/Apple).
*   **Grip Strength:** Use a dynamometer or simply "Hanging for Time" (stability/strength metric).
*   **HRV & Resting HR:** Track trends via wearable to measure stress/recovery balance.

## Clinical Script for Requisitions
*"I'm tracking my metabolic and cardiovascular health using a longevity-focused framework. I'd like to pay for an ApoB and Lp(a) test to better stratify my lipid risk, and I need a repeat hs-CRP and Ferritin now that I'm over that recent viral illness."*
