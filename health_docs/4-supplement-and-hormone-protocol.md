# Supplement and Hormone Protocol

Current management strategy for testosterone optimization and general micronutrient support.

## 1. Testosterone Support (Micro-Dosing Protocol)
*   **Medication:** Clomiphene (Clomid).
*   **Current Dose:** 25mg every 3 days.
*   **Rationale:** A conservative "slow-start" to minimize CNS side effects (headaches, anxiety, fatigue) experienced during the initial trial/illness overlap.
*   **Half-Life Note:** Clomid has a ~5-7 day half-life; E3D dosing provides a stable low-level exposure.

## 2. Micronutrient Regimen
*   **Vitamin D:** 2,500 IU daily (Reduced from 10,000 IU during initial trial).
*   **B-Complex:** Methylated multivitamin (contains methylcobalamin).
*   **Note on Methylation:** Watch for "over-methylation" signals (wired-but-tired, increased anxiety).

## 3. "Stop Signals" (Attia-Style Monitoring)
Hormone optimization is secondary to quality of life. Immediate reasons to pause or adjust:
*   Significant decline in sleep quality.
*   Increased irritability or "emotional flattening."
*   Persistent low-grade headaches (a known Clomid side effect).
*   Worsening anxiety.

## 4. Hierarchy of Priority
1.  **Sleep & Stress Management** (Foundational)
2.  **Training & Stability** (Longevity driver)
3.  **Metabolic/Lipid Metrics** (Risk mitigation)
4.  **Hormonal Optimization** (Quality of Life)
