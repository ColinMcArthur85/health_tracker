# Cardiovascular and Inflammation Risk Analysis

This document outlines identified risk signals and data gaps regarding cardiovascular disease and chronic inflammation.

## 1. Inflammation (CRP)
**Signal: High (Single Point)**
*   **April 2025 Result:** 15.7 mg/L.
*   **Attia Framing:** CRP > 10 mg/L usually indicates acute infection or injury rather than chronic "smoldering" inflammation. However, chronic inflammation accelerates cardiovascular disease independent of cholesterol.
*   **Context:** This was likely transient noise due to acute illness (see Clinical Context doc).

## 2. White Blood Cell (WBC) & Neutrophils
**Signal: Intermittent Elevations**
*   **Pattern:** Recurrent elevations in 2021 and 2025.
*   **Attia Framing:** Suggests intermittent immune activation. In the context of parenting young kids and sleep deprivation, this is likely reactive rather than pathological.

## 3. Lipid Profile
**Signal: Incomplete Information**
*   **Standard Results:** Total Cholesterol ~5.0 mmol/L, Triglycerides and HDL are good.
*   **The Problem:** Standard LDL-C is a weak proxy for cardiovascular risk.
*   **Missing Data (Attia "Must-Haves"):**
    *   **ApoB:** The total number of atherogenic particles.
    *   **Lp(a):** A highly atherogenic, genetically determined particle (test once in a lifetime).
    *   **LDL Particle Number (LDL-P):** If ApoB is unavailable.

## 4. Ferritin Trend
**Signal: Rising (50 to 121 µg/L)**
*   **Observation:** Ferritin more than doubled between 2021 and 2025.
*   **Attia Framing:** Ferritin is an "acute-phase reactant." A rise often mirrors inflammation (CRP) or metabolic stress rather than pure iron overload.
*   **Status:** Needs re-testing alongside hs-CRP to confirm it returns to baseline (40-60 range).
